Where the checklist comes from

The 9 items marked with a percentage are the information types that Directors of Nursing most often reported as missing, in a survey covering 471 hospital–SNF pairs: Adler-Milstein J, Raphael K, O'Malley TA, Cross DA. Information Sharing Practices Between US Hospitals and Skilled Nursing Facilities to Support Care Transitions. JAMA Netw Open. 2021;4(1):e2033980. Percentages for the first six are from the article text; the last three are read from its Figure 2B. The other items are standard transition-of-care content.

Detection uses word patterns, so it can miss information written in an unusual way or count a passing mention. Treat it as a second pair of eyes, not a replacement for reading the summary. Sample summaries are synthetic.

Not a medical device. Built by a clinician as a prototype for SNF admissions teams.
